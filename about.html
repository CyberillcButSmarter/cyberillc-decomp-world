About

What this site is and why it exists.

Cyberillc's Decomp World is a personal project dedicated to decompiling and preserving Unity-based games. The goal is to make source code and assets accessible for study, modding, and historical preservation.

Why decompile?

Decompilation is a useful technique for understanding how games work under the hood — whether for learning, modding, or preserving games that would otherwise be lost. Everything here is shared for educational purposes.

Who runs this?

This site is run by Cyberillc. If you have questions or want to get in touch, find me on Discord: @cyberillc
