AB2 Plus Legacy

Unity Coming soon

A mod of Angry Birds 2 (2.9.0), with an account system called Re-Achieve.

What it is

ABUP Plus is built on the soft launch build of the game. This one is built on Angry Birds 2 itself, version 2.9.0, so it is the later and fuller version of the same game rather than the classic one.

The first release runs completely offline. Ads work, there is no sign in yet, and accounts are switched on later from the server, so you will not need to download a new build when they open.

ABUP Plus or AB2 Plus Legacy?

Neither replaces the other. ABUP Plus is the one to play if you want the early game with the pay to win filed off, and it is available today.

Re-Achieve

The account system built for this mod. When it opens it brings cloud saves that follow you between devices, level and Arena leaderboards, friends, gifts and achievements.

It is closed at launch on purpose. The mod goes out first so bugs get found in the game itself, and accounts open once that has settled. Nothing you play in the meantime is lost.

What about AB2 Plus?

A separate mod, based on a much newer version of Angry Birds 2. It is not out and there is no date. It is waiting on the decompiler to finish 2.15.1, and until that is done there is nothing to build against.
