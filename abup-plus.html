Changelog

v1.1.3
September 18, 2026
  • A warning before a save is replaced — backing up or restoring over a save that holds progress the other one lacks now opens a comparison screen first, showing both side by side: level, stars, levels done, pigs, gems, birds, spells and arena league. When neither is ahead, nothing changes.
  • Auto Save — the game already synced with the cloud by itself; now it says so, and says when it failed. A switch on the account screen turns it off, leaving Back Up and Restore as the only things that touch your cloud save.
  • Undo a bad backup — every backup keeps the save it replaced, and Re-Achieve's dashboard can put it back.
  • Fixed being signed out by a server outage — only a genuine refusal clears your session now. If it really did expire, the account screen says so.
  • Fixed a failed cloud upload being recorded as a successful one.
v1.1.2.1
September 14, 2026
  • Fixed the sign-in code screen — the account screen's buttons stayed drawn on top of it, and still worked. Affected every popup opened over another.
v1.1.2
September 13, 2026
  • Accounts and cloud saves through Re-Achieve — back up and restore your progress, using the same account as AB2 Plus Legacy.
  • Sign-in screen that shows the browser code on its own page.
  • Use your Re-Achieve name and picture in game, with a way back to the previous one.
  • Graphics options — resolution scale, texture quality, anti-aliasing.
  • Safe-area support, a working Android back button, and no gaps where a hidden leaderboard used to be.
  • Android 4.2 and up (was 5.0).
  • Fixed buttons growing on tap, unusable sliders, and the missing signed-in name.
  • Removed the startup resolution dialog on desktop — the graphics menu replaces it.
v1.1.1
May 1, 2026
  • Fixed Options menu scaling.
  • Fixed room counter when boss bar is at the bottom.
v1.1.0
April 30, 2026
  • Added two new smaller UI scale options: Micro and Tiny.
  • Added an option to move the boss health bar to the bottom of the screen, suggested by Bow! (@yasmiiin_24) on Discord.
  • Made Terence not one-shot the Boss.
v1.0.9
April 30, 2026
  • Added new loading tips by Bow! (@yasmiiin_24) on Discord.
  • New tips include: "Red's battlecry can deflect pig projectiles!", "Pigs may glide for a safe landing.", and "King Pig's favorite toys are teddy bears. Don't let your birds become one!"
  • Fixed pause menu bug appearing in arena on certain devices.
  • Hearts now refill faster (20 minutes instead of 30).
  • Starting hearts increased from 5 → 10.
v1.0.8
April 28, 2026
  • First "actual" patch.
  • Fixed the life countdown getting stuck at 30:00 by correcting the life timer timestamp handling and making the countdown update reliably in menus/popups.
  • Fixed Terrence being too bouncy since his velocity during his 1.75 "smash" ability was too fast.
  • Balanced the arena and bird levels.
  • Added 4 new tips by Giovanni (@giovanni_ab) on Discord (Thanks!) and 11 new tips by me.
  • New tips include: "Chuck is fast than Usain Bolt", "Bomb explodes", "Terence and Red are brothers", and "Did you know Bomb can explode like a pig?" (changed to "chili pig").
v1.0.7
April 28, 2026
  • Added loading tips.
  • Red's Battle Cry is now buffed! his ability scales with each upgrade!
  • Added more feathers across levels. If you haven't collected them, go back!
  • Terence's Smashing ability now triggers on impact and his velocity won't slow down until the 1.75s window ends. The tradeoff: he launches at 90% of his original speed.
  • Better Arena Rewards and lowered card level requirements.
v1.0.6
April 26, 2026
  • Added Gifs as profile images.
  • Improved the ad player internally.
  • Shop no longer appears when you don't have enough money.
  • Fixed Terrance stopping completely when hitting stone, i backported his AB2 buff making him powerful for 1.75 seconds, then returning to normal.
v1.0.5
April 25, 2026
  • Destruct-O-Meter fills 25% faster based on bird level.
  • Removed Analytics.
  • UI Scaling actually works now!
  • Fixed Sliver flying when hit mid-loop.
  • Profile images now work even offline! (Restart required after changing your pfp.)
  • Slight tweaks to the Ad Player.
v1.0.4
April 24, 2026
  • Sliver's buffed ability recreated from Angry Birds 2 with some script credit from Dive.cs. Her ability scales with each level-up. At Level 8 she's a danger to any pig nearby!
  • Options menu change your username and profile picture, toggle or disable custom ads, and a WIP UI scaling setting.
  • Pause ads ads now show in the pause menu.
  • Faster ad backend rebuilt using Vercel and a JSON manifest for much snappier ad loading.
